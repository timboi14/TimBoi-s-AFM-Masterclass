Spot the trap

  • NPV inflation: Real with real, nominal with nominal. NEVER mix.
  • Tax timing: 1-yr lag means tax CF appears one year after the operating profit. Last year extends one beyond project life.
  • Working capital: Incremental investment each year. RELEASE total at end of project.
  • FX bid/offer: Bank gives the worse rate to you. Importer (buying $) gets the LOW $/£; exporter (selling $) gets the HIGH $/£.
  • FRA: Borrower buys at OFFER (higher). Settlement on the difference, principal does NOT change hands.
  • Swap QSD: Side with bigger absolute fixed advantage borrows fixed; the other floats.
  • APV: Base @ ungeared Ke. Tax shield + subsidy @ Kd (pre-tax). Subsidy benefit must be (1−T).
  • Real options: Pa = PV inflows. Pe = capex (or scrap for abandon). σ from industry analogue.
  • VaR: z(95%)=1.645, z(99%)=2.326. T-day = 1-day × √T.
  • Valuation: FCFF→WACC→EV→subtract debt. FCFE→Ke→equity. Don\'t double-discount.
