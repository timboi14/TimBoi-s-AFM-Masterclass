User explicitly framed a redesign as wanting the work of a designer "who has worked for Google, Apple and Anthropic."

Implications for design work in this repo:
- Restraint over decoration: calibrated whites/grays, generous whitespace, tight typography rhythm.
- Cinematic but not flashy motion: scroll-driven, parallax, easing curves like [0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1].
- Premium tokens (gradients, glass, soft shadows) but never at the cost of legibility.
- Comfortable mixing the football/sports DNA with high-end finish — keep the personality.
- Considers "AI inside" (voice, dictation, in-app coach) a first-class differentiator, not a gimmick.
