# AFM classroom

Open **index.html** in Chrome or another modern browser. Keep this folder together so the linked deck, source papers and reading pages remain available. The classroom is local and has no sign-in, paid API or external hosting.

Begin with Lessons & challenges. Choose a lesson, attempt its question, then reveal a hint or teaching checkpoint. The 42-question library opens every original requirement and full exhibit set. In Question studio, save a written or spoken-answer note, confidence, an error and a retry date. Use Voice companion to prepare the current question for an existing Codex voice conversation.

Progress stays in the browser and can differ between a file view and a local preview. Use **Save progress backup** before changing browser, moving devices or clearing browser data. **Restore backup** merges saved records into the current browser. The CSV is an independent printable tracker and does not synchronise automatically.

- `materials/study-plan.html`: eight focused hours Saturday–Wednesday, optional ninth hour, lighter Thursday.

- `materials/question-guide.html`: original requirements and staged teaching routes.
- `materials/voice-tutor.html`: adaptive tutor workflow and continuity prompt.

The classroom distinguishes its teaching calculations, ACCA examiner commentary and third-party answer links. Coverage and confidence are not a guarantee of passing.

## Voice and reading

Open **index.html** for Classroom or **classroom14.html** for academy typography. They share the same questions and progress.

Use **Read full question** to hear every scenario paragraph, exhibit and requirement in source order. Choose an exhibit in the adjacent menu to jump directly to it. Read buttons beside text, and selected-text reading, cover lesson content, revealed hints, reference pages and tables. The Voice & reading panel provides pause, resume, stop, voice preview, available voices and rate.

Start microphone is explicit and may need browser permission. Choose Commands for navigation and study actions, or Dictation plus a named text field for notes and answers. Rich text is supported in the academy CBE editor. Commands never insert answer text. Reading turns the microphone off; starting Commands pauses this reader so “resume reading” can continue it with the microphone off. Speech support and available voices vary by browser and device. Browser recognition may need an online service and may be unavailable from a local file; keyboard input remains available.

These are browser speech tools, not an additional conversational AI tutor. Use the supplied handover prompt with your existing tutor for adaptive teaching.
