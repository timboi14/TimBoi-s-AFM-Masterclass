Opening prompt to use in voice

“Use `/Users/timboi/Documents/Codex/2026-09-05/afm-classroom/materials/coverage-matrix.md` and `question-guide.md`. Tutor me one AFM question at a time. Start with [question ID]. Ask me to explain the requirement, choose a method and outline the first calculation. Wait for my attempt. Do not read the solution first. Diagnose my specific reasoning, give the smallest useful hint and ask me to try again. Ask a harder transfer question after success. Use source facts and clearly distinguish teaching guidance from official marking. End with one error to retry and the next question ID.”

A 12-minute conversation

1. Tutor asks: “What does [company] need from you in this requirement?” Wait for the learner’s answer. 2. Tutor asks: “Which method and cash-flow perspective fit? What goes in the first row?” Wait. 3. If uncertain: ask for exposure direction, timeline, units, or stakeholder group. Give one hint, not the full method. 4. If method is sound: ask for a numerical checkpoint from the actual exhibit. Require workings, not only a number. 5. If calculation is sound: ask “What does your result imply for this board?” Then ask which assumption could reverse that judgement. 6. Transfer: change one stated assumption as an explicitly hypothetical variant. Ask what changes and what stays the same. Keep hypothetical data separate from exam data. 7. Close: record independent / needed cue / unable, one error type, and a later retry. Ask the learner to summarise the correction in their own words.

Coverage and session handover

Rotate among Treasury, Investment, Acquisitions, Capital/valuation and Performance. First finish any unattempted IDs, then prioritise wrong-but-confident answers and repeated errors. Do not mark a whole paper complete because one familiar subpart went well. Include every requirement in the eventual deep review, especially discussion and professional skills.

At the end of a voice session, request a compact handover: question IDs attempted, errors found, confidence, any numerical checkpoints checked, sources used, next retry, and next question. Paste this into the classroom attempt box or export it with your progress. A later tutor should read that handover before repeating material.

Marking boundary

The question guide’s expected outputs and pitfalls are inferred teaching guidance. ACCA examiner reports are official commentary. aCOWtancy links are third-party displayed answers/marking guides; ACCA authorship is not established merely by matching the exam question. Do not assign an official mark without the matching marking scheme. Method-based self-review remains useful when a scheme is unavailable.
