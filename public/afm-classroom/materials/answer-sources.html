What is authoritative

Original requirements: copied source files. Official general examiner expectations: the cited ACCA pages/report. aCOWtancy answer text: third-party displayed solution, provenance qualified. Classroom methods, examples and scoring prompts: teaching guidance. Confidence, completion and readiness checks are learning diagnostics, not marks or a passing guarantee.
