Your AFM study plan: 5–11 September 2026

Wednesday 9 September is the readiness milestone. The exam is Friday 11 September, as you confirmed. Use your admission details for the exact exam time and arrangements.

What the hours mean

The core is eight focused hours each day Saturday–Wednesday. Breaks and meals are additional. The optional ninth hour is gentle retrieval, never a reason to sacrifice sleep. Fourteen complete 195-minute papers would take 45.5 hours before any marking. The first four days therefore use meaningful exposure to every question, selected deep attempts and one timed rehearsal. This is coverage and skill-building, not a promise of complete mastery.

A workable eight-hour clock: 08:00–10:00 (two 60-minute sessions), 10:20–12:20, 13:20–15:20, 15:40–17:40. Break for 20 minutes mid-morning, 60 minutes at lunch and 20 minutes mid-afternoon. Within each block, briefly stand/stretch as needed and extend the finish if you want exactly eight net focused hours. Optional review: 19:00–20:00, only if rested. Move the clock to suit your start time.

Every question gets an active first pass

Use 12 minutes per question: 3 minutes to map its requirements to exhibits, 4 minutes for a calculation setup or numerical checkpoint, 3 minutes to draft one case-based paragraph, 2 minutes to record an error and confidence. A first pass does not count as a solved paper. Mark “attempted” only after producing something. Mark “retrieved” only after a later attempt without the notes.
